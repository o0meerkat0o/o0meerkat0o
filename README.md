### About me

I'm a Cornell double major in Computer Science and Electrical & Computer Engineering with a minor in Applied Mathematics. I'm most interested in **embedded systems, hardware, and machine learning**, and in the places where they meet. I'm from Seattle.

### What I've been working on

- **Cornell Electric Vehicles (Telemetry):** low-level drivers and tooling for CAN-FD communication and a live dashboard
- **Undergraduate research:** machine learning and information-theory projects in a Cornell lab
- **Computer vision:** an image classifier for Monarch butterfly life stages
